Why this module

The date filter of the Odoo accounting reports offers month, quarter, year and custom dates. To audit an account, reconcile an old partner balance or answer an accountant's question, you need its whole history, and that means guessing the date of the first entry and typing two dates by hand. Every time.

This module adds the missing entry: All history.

One click

A new entry in the date filter of every period report, right after Year.

Always complete

The range runs from the first to the last journal item and is recomputed on every reload: a new entry, even dated in the past, is never left out.

Zero footprint

No model, no setting, no data written. Uninstall it and the reports are exactly as before.

FAQ

Does it slow down large databases?
No. The range is found with a single MIN/MAX query on the journal item date, an indexed column, whatever the size of the database. The report itself is then computed by Odoo exactly as for custom dates.
Does it change any data?
No. Nothing is written in the database: the module only adds a choice to the date filter. Uninstalling it leaves no trace.
Which companies are included?
The companies currently selected in the company switcher, like every other filter of the reports.
Can I still compare periods?
Yes. The comparison, journal and posted/draft filters work with All history exactly as with custom dates.
Is there anything to configure?
No. Install it and the entry is there for every user who can open the reports.
